    - 10 / 22 - CRAFT THE RAFT

    RAFTS

    • - NEW Raft (30 wood, workbench): a floating tile. Place it on the water, then add tiles beside it to build a base that sails
    • - Your tiles join only your rafts and the rafts of your team, never the raft of another player
    • - Build on your raft: walls, doors, spikes, chests, fires, furnaces, beds, totems, towers...
    • - On your raft, build on the grid or off it, like on land (G to switch): on the grid, one building in the middle of each tile; off the grid, right where you aim, as many as fit. The ship wheel, the plots and the wooden towers always go in the middle of their tile
    • - Cover it with roofs, stone roofs and windows, over your buildings: they sail with your raft, keep you warm and out of the storms, and a house of stone on your raft hides you like on land
    • - Climb a wooden tower on your raft: from up there your arrows fly over the walls
    • - Farm at sea: place a plot on your raft and plant any seed on it. Your crops sail with you, and a window over them makes them grow faster
    • - NEW Ship wheel (80 wood, 4 cords, 5 gold, workbench): place it on your raft, click the button over it and steer with your movement keys. The wheel turns and the raft follows. On private servers, give it with ship-wheel
    • - A raft never outruns a diver: even with the pirate hat, it sails no faster than a player in a diving suit. Past 6 tiles, the more tiles, the slower your raft
    • - Wear the pirate hat at the wheel: your raft sails 9% faster. A true captain!
    • - Rafts sail on the sea, the lakes and the rivers: a raft of 3 x 3 tiles fits in a river one tile wide, and no raft goes more than one tile onto the land. A small raft stays on its river, never on the grass beside it
    • - Dock your rafts: steer a small raft into a gap of a bigger one (an I in a U), it slides in on its own. Docked, it sails with the big one, crew included, until you sail it out
    • - Bridges slow down the rafts that sail over them
    • - On a raft you don't swim, and the current of a river no longer carries you
    • - Only the hammers break the tiles. A broken tile drops what stands on it in the water, and a raft cut in two sails as two rafts

    PUPPET COLLECTION

    • - NEW Puppet collection: 31 skins, 30 crates and 6 books
    • - Farmer, Builder, Warrior and Shaman Puppets, with their winter, desert, ocean, lava and jungle versions, one rarity above the classic ones
    • - Strange little wooden starvers... Some say the strings that move them are not cut yet, and that one day they will wake up. Those who own them on that day may be glad they did. Better get them now!

    LEADERBOARD & PROFILE

    • - NEW Leaderboard: a new panel, the game modes in a list with their icons, and the top 1000 players instead of 200. The YOU button finds you, even far down
    • - NEW Profile: your level, your progress to the next one and a road of rewards, with a badge every 5 levels up to level 60
    • - At level 3, place a building every 1 second

    SETTINGS

    • - NEW Uncap FPS in the graphics settings: the game draws as many frames as your computer can, and sends your keys sooner
    • - NEW Show FPS and Show Position: your frames per second and the tile you stand on, under the leaderboard
    • - NEW Transparent Flying Mount: your flying mount turns see-through, so you can see the ground below
    • - All of them are off by default

    GIANT TREES

    • - NEW Giant trees in the forest and the jungle, so big you can walk under their leaves
    • - Hide under them: the players outside the tree can't see you, even your teammates. Only the players under the same tree see each other
    • - Walk under the leaves and they fade, so you see who hides there with you. Flying or on a tower, you are above the leaves: no hiding there
    • - Their trunks give wood, and in the autumn the forest trees turn red and orange

    MAPS OF ANY SHAPE

    • - The biomes are no longer just rectangles: NEW polygon biomes, islands, coasts, bays and lakes of any shape, with their own beaches and waves
    • - Choose which edges of a biome have a beach, and how wide it is
    • - The biomes now reach the borders and the corners of the map, and you can build there
    • - Draw them yourself in the map editor of the guide: click the corners, drag them, move the whole biome with its resources. It can also copy, paste and rotate a selection

    GAMEPLAY

    • - A lost connection now comes back in a few seconds, to the same server, instead of a frozen game
    • - A kill by an effect (poison, bleeding, burning...) now counts for the player who gave it
    • - The quests show their description in a new tooltip
    • - The mobs stay in their biome: no more bears, foxes or penguins in the dragon cave, and the hawks and the parrots no longer fly far over the sea. The ones that chase you out of their biome still do, and the crabs still follow their king

    - 10 / 11 - THE WORLD BITES BACK

    EFFECTS IN THE GAME

    • - The world now gives effects by itself. They are light: a few points of life or of a gauge, a small slow for a few seconds, and they never stop you from fighting back or building
    • - Raw meat and raw fish can make you sick: cook them! Spiders and sandworms can poison you, wolves and spikes can make you bleed: while you bleed, every hit hurts more
    • - The blizzard can chill you, the sandstorm can blow sand in your eyes, the flames of a campfire burn you, the lava burns you and chokes you with ash, and out of oxygen you can drown
    • - Mammoths can make you dizzy, the dragon can freeze you, the lava dragon can burn you or choke you, and the kraken can terrify you
    • - Good ones too: sleeping in a bed heals you faster (but you get a bit hungry and wake up groggy), a bandage can heal you more, a cake or a pumpkin cake can make you regenerate
    • - The peasant clothes and the pitchforks can make your plants grow faster: the golden pitchfork more often, the winter peasant even faster
    • - Wear the orange crown: now and then luck smiles on you. For 20 seconds you are Lucky, and each pirate chest you break gives you the better of 2 items
    • - Each craft can make you Inspired: gears turn around your head, and for a minute your crafts are 25% faster. Craft again to keep the ideas coming!

    PROTECT YOURSELF

    • - What you wear on your head can protect you: a helmet against bleeding and dizziness, warm clothes against the blizzard and the frost of the dragon, a turban or the pilot hat against the sand
    • - The lava gear protects you from fire and ash, the diving gear from drowning, and a pirate fears no kraken
    • - Your head holds only one: in the winter, choose between a warm hat and a helmet!
    • - On fire? Jump in the water: it puts the fire out at once
    • - Keep the mouse pressed on the icon of an effect: it tells you what protects you from it and where it stops

    SETTINGS

    • - NEW Particles option in the graphics settings: turn off the particles of the effects. They are on by default, and the icons of your effects stay

    PRIVATE SERVERS

    • - These effects are the new defaults of the Effects section of the guide: change them, turn them off or add yours
    • - NEW triggers: a gauge between two values (out of oxygen, low life, too hot...), a chest opened (a pirate chest, a gift), a craft (one item or any item) and a roof over the player
    • - NEW Only with an item: a trigger can ask for what the player wears, holds or rides, like a gift that only charms the players in a christmas hat
    • - NEW Only after a while: a place, a building, a mount, what the player wears or a gauge can wait until the player has been there a few seconds in a row before it gives an effect, like 5 seconds in the water or a helmet worn for 4 seconds. Step out and it starts again
    • - NEW Luck: an effect can make the pirate chests give the better of 2 items, like Lucky
    • - NEW Protected by: the hats and helmets that lower the chance of an effect, with the sets of the game in one click (helmets, warm clothes, desert, lava and diving gear)
    • - NEW Stopped by: where the player goes to end an effect, the water, the lava, a roof, a biome, a building, a storm, a mount, what it wears or a gauge. Burning stops in the water
    • - NEW Stopped by food, a drink or a bandage: the effect ends as soon as the player eats, drinks or uses it, like a bandage that stops the bleeding. The icon of the effect says Cured by, and Food & bandages shows what each item gives and stops
    • - NEW particles: gears that turn, two by two meshing, with the icon of a starver reading a book
    • - FIX: back on the guide, the effects you never touched no longer show as changed with errors
    • - FIX: the sliders of an effect no longer jump when they reach none, and their track is wider on a computer
    • - To configure your server, go on https://starve.io/commands.html

    - 10 / 06 - AUTOMATION & BALANCING

    AUTO CRAFT

    • - Press C (or Auto Craft in the settings): you craft your last item again and again, as long as you can
    • - It stops when you run out of resources, leave the workbench or the fire, or your inventory is full. Craft one by hand to start again
    • - Press C again to stop: the item you are crafting still ends

    AUTO RECYCLE

    • - Press V (or Auto Recycle in the settings): you recycle your last recycled item again and again, at a workbench, until you have none left
    • - No faster than by hand: one item at a time

    AUTO FEED

    • - Auto feed now keeps you fed while you craft or recycle, so auto craft and auto recycle never starve you
    • - Fixed auto ice: it eats 1 ice every 5 seconds (one tick of the gauges) instead of one every 2 seconds, no more wasted ice
    • - Hungry with nothing to eat? Auto feed still drinks and eats ice

    BALANCING

    • - Blue Crown: the stronger the weapons and spikes you carry, the less time your ghost has to resurrect. Nothing changes up to gold, then topaz 45 s, diamond 35 s, amethyst 25 s, reidite 15 s, sapphire, jade, ruby, coal and aquamarine 12 s, emerald 10 s, dragon 8 s, lava 5 s. Your strongest item counts
    • - An item still counts for 30 s after it leaves your inventory (placed, dropped, put in a chest...)
    • - When it makes you a ghost, you lose all your weapons, shields and spikes (axes and wands count as weapons)
    • - You keep your pickaxes and tools up to reidite, the better ones are lost. Order: wood, stone, copper, iron, gold, topaz, diamond, amethyst, reidite, then sapphire, jade, ruby, coal, aquamarine, emerald
    • - Dragon Heart: it can only save you once per life. A new heart found after your resurrection won't save you again before your next life
    • - Wearing the blue crown when you die? The crown saves you and your heart is kept for later
    • - Vampires ride every mount and vehicle 15% faster

    PRIVATE SERVERS

    • - NEW Coal boost for the extractors: set it to x2 and players can put coal in an extractor to make it work twice as fast
    • - It burns 1 coal per resource and still needs its wood. Off by default (x1), in the Buildings of the guide
    • - Choose the items lost with the blue crown, and with the dragon heart, in the Survival of the guide. Back to default now works too
    • - Choose the ghost time each item gives with the blue crown, how long the crown remembers them (30 s by default), or turn it off, in the Survival of the guide
    • - How many times a dragon heart can save a player in one life (0 to 100, 1 by default)
    • - The speed bonus of the vampires on mounts (0 to 200%, 15% by default), in Speed & mounts
    • - To configure your server, go on https://starve.io/commands.html

    - 10 / 04 - POWERING THE TOWERING

    NEW TOWERS

    • - Four new towers to protect your base... or to attack the others!
    • - Fuel a tower with coal and it lights up a circle around it: every second, it hurts, heals or repairs what is inside, as long as it has coal to burn
    • - To fuel it, stand next to it and click the + on your coal (SHIFT + click: 10, CTRL + click: 100). A tower holds up to 255 coal
    • - 1 coal lasts 30 seconds in a red or green tower and 20 seconds in an orange or blue one, even when nobody is in the circle
    • - Towers have 3000 HP and can be destroyed. Two towers of the same color don't add up on the same target
    • - Craft them at a workbench and a fire while standing in water, like the emerald machine
    • - Red Tower: hurts the enemy players in its circle (10 damage per second, helmets don't protect)
    • - Recipe: 20 Ruby + 80 Stone + 5 Flames
    • - Green Tower: heals you and your team in its circle (6 per second)

    • - Orange Tower: damages the enemy buildings in its circle (25 per second)
